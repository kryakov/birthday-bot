# Иллюстрация «Звёздный день»

Создана встроенным инструментом `image_gen.imagegen`. Исходная иллюстрация: `star-day.png`. Копия `avatar.jpg` подготовлена для Telegram без изменения композиции.

## Финальный промпт

Create a polished square illustration for a Russian Telegram discovery bot named «Звёздный день», used by university students to discover celebrities born on their birthday, play a quiz, and talk to a friendly local AI. Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: both a circular-crop-safe bot avatar and its welcome card. Primary subject: one charming little luminous five-point golden star character, wearing oversized dark blue headphones, sitting casually on a small rounded blue calendar block with a single small star symbol instead of numbers. One hand casually makes a peace gesture. Editorial 3D clay illustration, tactile matte materials, clean studio lighting, sophisticated rather than childish, relaxed playful student culture. Deep midnight-blue background with a gentle circular glow, a few tiny distant stars, clear uncluttered silhouette and generous margins for circular crop. Centered large character should read well at tiny avatar sizes. Warm cream and golden star with electric-blue accents, tasteful cinematic light. No text, no letters, no watermarks, no extra characters, no university logos, no brand logos. High quality square finished illustration.
